Incorporated as a town in 2025.

San Tan Valley is in Pinal County. Voters approved incorporation in August 2025, and the town was declared on September 17, 2025. Before that it was a census-designated place governed by the county, and the 2020 census counted 99,894 residents there.

A change like that does not alter how mice and rats behave, but it does cause paperwork confusion. Older listings, maps and neighbors may still call it unincorporated. For a visit, what matters is the address and the ZIP code. If a permit or community approval is needed for sealing work, ask which authority applies instead of assuming.

A short safety list before anyone cleans up

This is general public-health guidance from the CDC, not a description of any one job. It applies whether you call us or anyone else.

  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings or nests. That can put contaminated dust in the air you breathe.
  • Give the room time to ventilate. Thirty minutes with the doors open is the common recommendation.
  • Wear rubber or plastic gloves for the whole job, and scrub your hands with soap and water at the end.
  • Soak the droppings and the surface in disinfectant first. Wiping comes only after the soak time.
  • Put used towels and waste in a plastic bag, tie it closed and throw it away. Never touch a dead rodent without gloves.
  • Flu-like symptoms or shortness of breath after rodent exposure are a reason to see a doctor and mention the cleanup. Health questions belong with a physician, not a pest company.
